# Engineering Method 0.2.0

Released 2026-09-17. This minor release grows the shared skill set from 17 to
29 skills. It adds reader-oriented documentation work at three scales, Humanizer
guidance during drafting, local PDF export, engineering playbooks for
development operations and system deliverables, and a read-only backlog state
check. Existing lifecycle skills and the Claude, Codex and OpenCode adapters
keep their behavior.

## Changes

- Documentation lifecycle: `documentation-planning`, `documentation-authoring`
  and `documentation-review` decide the writing scale first, then plan, draft
  and check documentation sets under `docs/writing`.
- Supporting writing skills: `explaining-concepts`, `writing-procedures`,
  `empathic-troubleshooting`, `terminology-guard` and `visual-placeholders`
  apply in documentation and in ordinary replies.
- `human-prose-drafting` applies adapted Humanizer constraints from the first
  sentence of medium and large texts, without changing facts or structure.
- `documentation-pdf` exports Markdown to one local PDF through Quarto or Typst,
  with a reusable design, table of contents and page numbers.
- `playbook-authoring` and `playbook-review` add scenario-bound playbooks;
  Spec Kit and OpenSpec lifecycles assess playbook need, and stale or missing
  required evidence blocks the dependent operation, convergence or archival.
- `backlog state-check` is read-only: it detects remote availability without
  creating issues, replacing caches or deleting archives.
- Native evaluation preserves the host's own Claude authentication.

## Verification and acceptance

Local verification on the released source: 319 passing tests, plugin
validators, pinned-source provenance audit, independent reviews, a reproducible
committed-source package and disposable Claude/Codex installation without
credentials. Native routing was checked with five documentation cases on each of
Codex and Claude and two bounded OpenCode probes; see
[EM-013](../verification/EM-013-prepublication.md). Playbook integration has
local and review evidence only, without native routing evaluation; see
[EM-014](../verification/EM-014-playbooks.md). Sub-records
[EM-010](../verification/EM-010-documentation-skills.md),
[EM-011](../verification/EM-011-humanizer-drafting.md) and
[EM-012](../verification/EM-012-quarto-pdf.md) hold the per-feature evidence.

The routing probes are bounded selection and pre-draft checks; they do not
prove prose quality across a complete draft or a full documentation set. The
OpenCode discovery evidence still covers the 17 skills of 0.1.1 plus two later
probes; no full OpenCode discovery of all 29 skills is claimed. The
[historical 0.1.0 acceptance](../verification/EM-005-acceptance.md) and the
[0.1.1 OpenCode scope](0.1.1.md) retain their original limits. No new full
lifecycle, multi-agent or compaction acceptance is claimed.

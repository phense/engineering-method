# Defect Convergence

Every actionable review or verification finding remains open until current
evidence proves it resolved or a terminal blocker is recorded. A retry must add
new evidence, a new hypothesis, a changed approach, or a model or perspective
escalation; an identical retry brief is invalid.

Start with the original implementer and the precise finding. Require a
root-cause statement and a test that would catch recurrence. Use the narrowest
meaningful covering test after a fix, broadening at integration boundaries or
when the fix affects integration. Detailed escalation and terminal conditions
are owned by `orchestrated-implementation`.
